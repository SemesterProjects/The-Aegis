# Problem, Market, and Positioning

## Problem statement
Modern security systems suffer from several structural weaknesses:

1. Signature dependency: most detection is based on known malware patterns and static rules.
2. Alert fatigue: analysts are flooded with alerts and spend more time triaging than understanding.
3. Slow response: the detection-to-patch cycle often takes hours or days.
4. Separation of development and defense: security is often disconnected from runtime understanding.
5. Reactive AI: most systems recognize suspicious activity without explaining root cause or safe remediation.

## Why Aegis matters
The real need is not just detection; it is evidence-backed understanding and actionable mitigation. Security teams require the ability to move from alert to explanation to verified fix.

## Market thesis
The market lacks a product that compresses the full security lifecycle into one integrated workflow: observe, understand, investigate, reason, verify, approve, and recover.

## Competitive position
Aegis sits between the worlds of EDR/XDR, software security validation, and AI-driven security assistants. It differentiates itself by connecting runtime evidence with source-level understanding and mitigation verification.

## Positioning statement
Aegis is not a generic AI security copilot. It is a reasoning platform that produces explainable, evidence-driven, human-governed security actions.

## Differentiators
- Runtime-to-source causality mapping
- Explanation of why a behavior is suspicious
- Candidate mitigation ranking
- Safe validation before deployment
- Human approval as a core design principle

## Product wedge
The best initial wedge is a Linux-first, high-confidence incident investigation and software-hardening workflow rather than a massive enterprise suite from day one.
